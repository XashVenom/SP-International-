About

A merchant trader in Ghaziabad

SP International Pvt Ltd is a buyer–seller bridge for Indian spices and brass / art metal décor. We source, we show samples on request, and we quote.

We sit between the buyer and the Indian market. You send a brief. We check suppliers. We come back with a sample path and a written quote.

That is the whole model. We are not a factory and we do not pretend to be one. We are not a retail shop. We are a trading desk that keeps claims tight.

Two lines of work

Spices — culinary spices for food manufacturers, distributors, and importers. We lead with turmeric, red chilli powder, and cumin. Other spices are available on request.

Brass / art metal décor — Indian metal décor for home and gift buyers who want a sourced range, not a warehouse tour.

Sample-friendly

Samples are available on request. We would rather you see the lot than buy from a photograph. Cost and timing of samples are part of the quote conversation.
